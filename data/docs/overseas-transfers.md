# Overseas Transfers

## How much does an overseas transfer cost?
Each overseas telegraphic transfer costs S$10, plus any fees charged by the receiving bank.

## How long does an overseas transfer take?
Overseas transfers usually take 1 to 3 working days.

## Is there a cut-off time?
Transfers submitted after 4pm on a working day are processed on the next working day.

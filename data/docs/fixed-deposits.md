# Fixed Deposits

## What fixed deposit rates do you offer?
The current board rates are 2.60% p.a. for a 6-month tenure and 2.45% p.a. for a 12-month tenure.

## What is the minimum placement?
The minimum placement is S$1,000.

## Can I withdraw early?
Yes, but if you withdraw before maturity you will not earn any interest on that placement.

# Merlion Credit Cards

## What is the annual fee?
The annual fee is S$196.20 including GST. It is waived for the first year.

## What happens if I pay late?
A late payment fee of S$100 applies and interest of 27.8% p.a. is charged on the outstanding balance.

## What is the minimum income to apply?
You need an annual income of at least S$30,000.

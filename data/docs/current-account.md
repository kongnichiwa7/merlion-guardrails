# Merlion Current Account

## Is there a fall-below fee?
A fall-below fee of S$7 per month applies if your average daily balance is below S$3,000.

## How much do I need to open a Current Account?
The minimum initial deposit is S$1,000.

## Do you charge for cheque books?
Your first cheque book is free. Each additional cheque book costs S$20.

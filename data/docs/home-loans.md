# Home Loans

## What home loan rates do you offer?
Our 2-year fixed rate package is 2.75% p.a.

## Is there a lock-in period?
The fixed rate package has a lock-in period of 2 years.

## Is there a prepayment penalty?
Repaying in full during the lock-in period incurs a penalty of 1.5% of the amount repaid.

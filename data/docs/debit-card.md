# Merlion Debit Card

## How much does a replacement debit card cost?
A replacement debit card costs S$5.

## What is my daily spending limit?
The default daily spending limit is S$5,000. You can lower or raise it in the Merlion Bank app.

## Can I use my debit card overseas?
Yes. Overseas transactions carry a foreign currency fee of 3.25% of the transaction amount.

# Merlion Everyday Savings Account

## What interest does the Everyday Savings Account pay?
The base interest rate is 0.05% p.a. on your whole balance.

## How do I earn bonus interest?
Credit a salary of at least S$2,000 a month and you earn bonus interest of up to 2.10% p.a. on the first S$75,000 of your balance. Bonus interest is credited on the first business day of the following month.

## Is there a minimum balance?
There is no minimum balance and no fall-below fee for the Everyday Savings Account.
